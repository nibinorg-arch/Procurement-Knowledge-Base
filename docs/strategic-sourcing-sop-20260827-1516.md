# Strategic Sourcing SOP

A repeatable, vendor-neutral procurement playbook drawn from 17+ years of GCC sourcing practice. This is a general methodology and contains no client, supplier, or commercial data.

## 1. Define the requirement
Translate the business need into a precise specification, target quality level, and total-cost expectation before approaching the market.

## 2. Analyse the category & market
Map supply concentration, price drivers, lead times, and substitution risk. Decide where leverage actually sits.

## 3. Build the supplier shortlist
Qualify on capability, capacity, compliance, and continuity — not price alone.

## 4. Run a structured RFx
Use an apples-to-apples comparison sheet. Score on total cost of ownership, not headline unit price.

## 5. Negotiate on value
Anchor on TCO levers: payment terms, logistics, warranty, and volume commitments.

## 6. Award, onboard & govern
Lock service levels, define escalation, and schedule performance reviews from day one.
